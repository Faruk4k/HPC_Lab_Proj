For SPMV, the most influential IMP parameter is the stream/lookahead distance, represented by the s or sdist setting. Across all four configurations, increasing this value from s2 to s8 and s16 consistently improves performance. The best results are obtained by the s16 family of configurations, reaching 1.784x for L1D + DDR4_1x, 1.675x for L1D + DDR4_2x, 1.713x for L2 + DDR4_1x, and 1.621x for L2 + DDR4_2x. Other parameters such as ipd, addr, dist, pt, and sth have only minor effects once the main s value is fixed. This indicates that SPMV mainly benefits from longer prefetch lookahead rather than fine-grained secondary tuning.
